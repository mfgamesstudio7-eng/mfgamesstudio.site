Privacy Policy

Information about how this website handles visitor information.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the MF Games Studio informational website at mfgamesstudio.site. Individual games or apps may have separate privacy policies covering their specific data practices.

Information You Provide

Our website does not currently provide user accounts, online purchases, or a contact form. If you choose to contact us by email, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

Website Hosting and Technical Data

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit, the hosting provider and network services may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, for website delivery, security, and operational purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for more information.

Cookies and Analytics

We do not currently integrate advertising trackers or analytics scripts into this website. External services involved in delivering website resources, such as Google Fonts, may receive technical connection information when your browser loads those resources.

How We Use Information

Information voluntarily sent to us by email may be used to respond to inquiries, provide support, and communicate about the subject of your message.

Sharing and Retention

We do not sell information submitted to us by email. Email providers and other service providers may process information as needed to provide their services. We retain correspondence only as reasonably needed for communication, business records, or applicable legal requirements.

Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended to provide general information about our studio. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through this website. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website and services evolve. The latest version will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit our Contact Us page.

Before publishing your games

This is a website-only privacy policy. If your mobile games use ads, analytics, in-app purchases, or other SDKs, create accurate game-specific privacy disclosures before publishing or linking this policy in Google Play Console.
